Category 08 · Layer 7 — Adoption and Customer Success

Customer Success & Adoption

Retention and expansion are where most of a SaaS company's revenue actually lives after year one — yet it's the layer most "AI-powered" CS platforms still treat as a health-score dashboard. We looked for the same thing we look for everywhere else on this site: can an agent create a task, update a record, or trigger a play on its own, through a scoped and auditable interface — or is the AI just narrating what a CSM should do next?

Some links below are affiliate links — see our full disclosure. Where a vendor has no self-serve affiliate program, we link straight to their site with no tracking and say so.
01Gainsight
High — native MCP, real write actions No self-serve affiliate link

Customer success & product experience platform — custom / enterprise pricing · gainsight.com

The category leader for enterprise and mid-market CS teams running health scores, CTAs, and playbooks, now pushing hard into "AI-native" retention workflows via its Atlas AI agent suite. The most substantive, best-documented agentic architecture of any vendor in this category.

Agentic angle: A first-party MCP connector lets an agent create and update CTAs, Success Plans, Timeline entries, and tasks — real, scoped write access via OAuth, not just a chat panel. Two honest caveats: it can't yet trigger a playbook or launch outreach on its own, and Gainsight's own docs describe a customer-facing audit report as a "planned capability," not something shipped yet.

02Vitally
High — native MCP, no write-confirmation gate Affiliate program

Customer success platform for PLG & mid-market SaaS — custom pricing · vitally.io

A flexible, highly customizable CS platform built for tech-touch through high-touch motions, popular with product-led SaaS teams that want CS workflows wired directly into product usage data.

Agentic angle: An official Vitally MCP server lets an agent create notes, tasks, and custom object records directly. The one governance gap worth flagging: Vitally's own docs note write actions have no built-in confirmation prompt — "review before you confirm, especially for customer-facing records" — worth knowing before you scope an agent's access.

03Planhat
High — native read-write MCP No self-serve affiliate link

Customer platform — CRM + CS + PSA — custom pricing · planhat.com

A flexible, data-centric customer platform spanning CRM, CS, and professional-services modules, aimed at teams that want to unify usage, health, and revenue data in one place rather than stitching CS on top of a separate CRM.

Agentic angle: An explicitly read-write MCP server — Planhat's own docs describe an agent that can "not only read real-time customer context... but also take action: creating tasks, updating records and building documents and dashboards." Governance is described only at a high level, and there's no detailed audit-trail documentation for MCP-initiated writes yet.

Medium — marketing-led, unconfirmed in docs Affiliate program

A CS and product/digital-adoption platform built around real-time engagement data and in-app guidance, aimed at mid-market to enterprise teams running renewal and expansion motions.

05Catalyst (Totango)
Limited — API writes, no MCP No affiliate program

Outcome-driven customer success — custom / enterprise pricing · totango.com

Catalyst merged into Totango in 2024 and now operates as a product line there, positioned around outcome-driven CS ("focus on critical business outcomes, not just adoption") for GTM/CS teams. Evaluate it as a Totango purchase, not an independent product.

Agentic angle: No first-party MCP server exists for Catalyst or the merged Totango entity — only third-party wrappers. The Catalyst API v1 is GET-only, but Totango’s HTTP, Touchpoints and SuccessPlay APIs do accept writes via API key, with no agent-specific scoping, confirmation step or audit trail. Verified against vendor documentation, 21 Aug 2026. The product page names a separate "Unison AI" feature with no detail behind it — the documented capability stays at outcome dashboards and recommendations a human still has to act on.
