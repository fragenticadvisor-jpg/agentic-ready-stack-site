Category 06 · Layer 3: Pricing and Packaging

Pricing & Packaging Strategy

This is the thinnest category on the site for affiliate purposes, and we're not going to dress that up. Price optimization software is bought by large manufacturers and distributors through enterprise sales cycles, not signed up for with a credit card: so treat everything below as informational, not a conversion path.

None of the vendors below are affiliate relationships. Every link goes straight to the vendor's own site, untracked. See our full disclosure. Note: an earlier version of our research shortlist included Monetizely, which has since repositioned itself from a self serve pricing tool into a pricing strategy consulting firm: we've dropped it from this comparison rather than review it as software it no longer is.
01Vendavo
Limited: no agent evidence No affiliate program

B2B price optimization & CPQ: custom / enterprise pricing · vendavo.com

AI/ML driven price optimization and rebate software for large manufacturers, distributors, and service enterprises with complex catalogs and customer segments, generating target and floor pricing guidance embedded into quoting workflows.

Agentic angle: Positioned as ML generated pricing recommendations surfaced to humans inside a workflow: we found no mention of agent APIs or third party agent connectivity anywhere in its product documentation.
Visit Vendavo →
02Zilliant
Limited: MCP announced, not documented No affiliate program

B2B price optimization & management: custom / enterprise pricing · zilliant.com

A long standing player in B2B price optimization and management, competing directly with Vendavo for large industrial and distribution accounts on similar ML driven pricing guidance capability.

Agentic angle: Zilliant issued a press release announcing an "industry first" MCP server slated for Q1 2026, but no MCP page appears anywhere in its public developer documentation or Customer API reference, so neither the scoping nor the write model can be checked. This is the exact pattern this site exists to flag: an agentic claim that lives in the newsroom rather than the docs. Verified against vendor documentation, 21 Aug 2026.
Visit Zilliant →
03PROS
Limited: no agent evidence No affiliate program

AI powered price optimization & CPQ: custom / enterprise pricing · pros.com

One of the oldest names in AI driven B2B pricing science, spanning price optimization, CPQ, and revenue management for large travel, manufacturing, and distribution enterprises.

Agentic angle: No evidence found of a public affiliate/reseller program, or of an agent facing API: like Vendavo and Zilliant, this is enterprise sales led pricing science, not an agent platform. Note also that PROS has divested its B2B CPQ and pricing line to Conga and now positions itself around airline retailing: if you are evaluating B2B pricing software, treat this entry as historical context and check where the product you want actually now sits. Verified against vendor documentation, 21 Aug 2026.
Visit PROS →
