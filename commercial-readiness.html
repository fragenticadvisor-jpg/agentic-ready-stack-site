Commercial Readiness

Eight layers of tooling. One layer no vendor sells.

This site reviews and scores vendors across eight of the nine layers of the Agentic Commercial Model — the operating-model framework originated by Fessal Rahman. The one layer it can't touch is Organisation Design: no amount of software fixes a commercial structure still built for the pre-agentic era. Below is a condensed map of all nine layers, and where the comparisons on this site fit inside each one.

The Agentic Commercial Model is the end-to-end commercial operating model for the agentic AI era — nine layers a software business has to rebuild once its customers, users, and competitors are deploying AI agents at scale. Its central argument: the commercial operating model, not the underlying technology, decides who wins the shift to agentic AI.

A business that buys every tool on this site and leaves Organisation Design untouched hasn't adapted. It has re-tooled.

The nine layers

  1. Organisation DesignWhether the commercial structure embodies the new operating model, rather than describing how work used to be done.
  2. Go-to-Market This siteReaching and winning the buyer who actually decides now — including the moment the buyer's own agents start shortlisting vendors.
  3. Pricing and Packaging This siteRebuilding the value metric so price scales with value delivered, not seats consumed.
  4. Sales Strategy and Enablement This siteEquipping teams to sell outcomes, and to answer why a customer should pay for this if the AI does the work.
  5. Compensation This siteRewarding the behaviour the AI strategy actually needs, so the comp plan pulls with the strategy instead of against it.
  6. Metering and Entitlement This siteMeasuring what the customer consumes and values — the precondition for usage- or outcome-based pricing.
  7. Adoption and Customer Success This siteCS structured and measured to drive and prove value realisation, not just manage renewals.
  8. Revenue Architecture and Financial Management This siteRecognition, forecasting, billing, and unit economics rebuilt for variable, consumption and outcome revenue.
  9. Commercial Systems and Data Infrastructure This siteThe CRM, CPQ, billing, and analytics stack capable of running the new model rather than blocking it — the layer every category on The Agentic Stack maps back to.
