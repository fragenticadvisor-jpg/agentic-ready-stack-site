Disclosure

Affiliate disclosure

The Agentic Stack participates in affiliate programs for some of the software we review. Where we're an approved affiliate and a live tracking link exists, clicking through and signing up for a paid plan may earn us a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Where a vendor doesn't run a public affiliate program — most enterprise, sales-led tools don't — we link straight to their own site with no tracking, and we tag the entry clearly as informational. We include those vendors because leaving out the honest best answer in a category just to keep every entry monetizable would make the comparisons worse, not better.

Vendors we're actively applying to or have an affiliate relationship with

HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Pipedrive, Semrush, Apollo.io, Qwilr, PandaDoc, Vitally, and ChurnZero all run public affiliate or partner-referral programs open to a site like this; we're in the process of applying to each. ClickFunnels is a live, tracked affiliate relationship today.

Vendors reviewed with no affiliate relationship

Ahoy, Klaviyo, Customer.io, Clay, Outreach, Gong, Highspot, Clari, DealHub, Vendavo, Zilliant, PROS, m3ter, DigitalRoute, Chargebee, Metronome, Orb, Gainsight, Planhat, Catalyst (Totango), CaptivateIQ, Everstage, QuotaPath, and Spiff are included for honest category coverage. Several of these — Gainsight and CaptivateIQ among them — run customer-referral bonus programs rather than a publisher affiliate program we could actually join, and we say so on the relevant category page rather than tagging them as an affiliate relationship. None of these links are tracked, and none of them pay us anything.

This is how the site funds the time it takes to actually test these tools rather than rewrite their marketing pages. It does not change our recommendations: tools are scored against the methodology described on our methodology page before we decide whether — or how — to link to them, and we turn down affiliate relationships with tools that don't hold up under that testing.

Not every link on this site is an affiliate link, and not every tool we mention has an affiliate program. Where a link is an affiliate link, that's disclosed at first mention in the article or vendor card, not just buried here.

This site is operated independently and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any vendor reviewed here, beyond the specific affiliate relationships described above.

Questions about this disclosure or a specific review can be sent to fessal@agenticreadystack.com.
