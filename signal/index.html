Signal

Weekly. Seven items. A link to the document behind every claim.

Most coverage of this market reprints vendor announcements. Signal reports what changed in the documentation — who shipped an agent interface, who moved off seats, who announced something the docs do not support — and tags each item to the layer of the Agentic Commercial Model it belongs to. When an item changes a vendor’s status, the registry row changes with it and carries a new date.

Quiet weeks get a short issue rather than a padded one. Nothing is included because it fills a slot.
